How to read this (and where it can mislead)

  • Cost per solved task = total spend ÷ passes. It charges a cheap model for its failures, and charges a reasoning model for its hidden thinking tokens.
  • Intervals are Wilson 95%. With 8 tasks × few trials they are wide; overlapping intervals mean “can't tell yet”, not “equal”.
  • Pass = hidden unit tests, single attempt. It ignores code quality, review effort, and multi-step agent behaviour; rerun on the customer's own repos and success criteria before drawing conclusions.
  • Latency includes network and queueing at the time of the run.
